The Sunderland Saga

Every old house has stories. Sunderland seems determined to keep creating new ones.

When I bought Sunderland in June 2025, I knew that an old house would come with a few challenges.

I just didn't realize how entertaining those challenges could be!

There was the television that required three different mounting solutions before finding one that worked. The skylight blind that needed a replacement cord of precisely the right thickness. The washing machine that arrived but couldn't fit through the doorway.

And then there were the windows that refused to open, the dining furniture that didn't quite live up to expectations, and the garden walkway that had practically disappeared beneath years of accumulated soil and mulch.

Some projects have been straightforward. Others have required considerable patience, ingenuity, a few new tools, and occasionally the assistance of family members.

I've learned that living in an old house requires a certain willingness to improvise—and a good sense of humor.

Not everything has gone according to plan. But even the frustrating experiences have provided stories worth remembering.

The Sunderland Saga is a collection of those stories.

Some are about solving problems. Some are about discovering the house's past. And some are simply about the peculiar little surprises that make Sunderland unlike anywhere else.

Come along for the adventures. There are plenty more chapters to come!
