Explore the House

From the street Sunderland seems like a modest cottage. Inside, it keeps finding more rooms—and more stories—to share.

The Living Room

This was love at first sight: warm pink walls, wood floors, tall windows and a fireplace. The room still feels like the heart of Sunderland.

The fireplace is not safe for wood fires, but a decorative tree-pattern screen gives it a beautiful presence. Even fitting the television above it turned into a Sunderland adventure.

The Reading Nook

One of Sunderland's unexpected little places to pause. The windows bring the outdoors close, especially when the trees put on their autumn colors.

Part of the delight of this house is discovering that there is always another corner just around the bend.

The Dining Room

A gathering place with furniture stories of its own. The table and chairs arrived after a first dining-room plan proved less successful than hoped.

The cabinets were originally chosen to match that first table—before the table moved to another house! Sunderland has a way of keeping its owner flexible.

The Kitchen

The kitchen changed before Pim moved in: upper cabinets were taken away to make room for artwork, leaving a brighter, more open space. Today, paintings hang where cabinets once stood.

It is an unusual kitchen for someone who loves to cook: plenty of worktop, a gas cooktop, and no conventional oven. There's a story behind almost every practical compromise.
